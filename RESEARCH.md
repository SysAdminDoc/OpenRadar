# Research: OpenRadar
Date: 2026-08-30. Replaces all prior research (the 2026-08-30 01:45 pass against v0.1.0 is superseded; every P0 it raised shipped in v0.2.0).

## Executive Summary

OpenRadar v0.2.0 (released 2026-08-30, tag v0.2.0, ~33,500 tracked lines: 24k TS/TSX, 5.9k Rust, 2.5k CSS) is now the only free, key-free, MIT desktop radar app that decodes MRMS, NEXRAD Level II, GOES GLM and GFS locally, ships a signed updater, works offline from a disk cache, and speaks Spanish. Its Rust decode lane is ahead of Supercell Wx (no MRMS, no satellite, issues #78/#512) and level with HookEcho on data breadth, while its MapLibre globe and command-bar UX are what neither of those has. The gap is in the analyst layer that every paid product and every serious OSS peer treats as table stakes on top of Level II: there is no storm-relative velocity, no per-product threshold filter, no storm cell attributes or arrival times, and no SPC outlooks or local storm reports. (Velocity dealiasing and downed-radar exclusion shipped on 2026-08-30.) The tracker is empty (zero issues, zero PRs, discussions off), so the evidence below comes from the code, the sibling repos, and the field.

Top opportunities, in order:
1. Trust fixes that cost little: one toast bypasses the catalogue and the 2.9 MB HURDAT file loads with no abort. (The README platform badge, the two stray OpenFreeMap hosts in the CSP, and the missing cancellation proof were fixed on 2026-08-30.)
2. Shipped 2026-08-30: a close-in view is now handed to the nearest site that has published a volume in the last twenty minutes, judged from the archive rather than the published station status.
3. Shipped 2026-08-30: region-based velocity dealiasing in `src-tauri/src/dealias.rs`. Storm-relative velocity from a VAD storm motion is the remaining half and still outstanding.
4. Storm cell attributes from Level III NST/NMD/NTV/NHI on `unidata-nexrad-level3` (flat bucket, keyless), giving tracks, motion, and "reaches the watched place in N minutes". Paywalled by Storm Radar ($19.99/yr), Baron, and RadarScope Tier 2; StormviewRadar already has a JS implementation to port.
5. SPC outlooks, mesoscale discussions and watches from the NWS CloudGIS ArcGIS services (no rate limit, no ToS), plus IEM local storm reports. StormScope holds proven adapters.
6. Per-product threshold filter, beam height at the cursor, per-layer opacity and reorder: the three Supercell Wx 2026 changes users asked for most (#480, #683, #691, #682).
7. ProbSevere (JSON on the MRMS bucket) and the wider MRMS catalogue (EchoTop, VIL, PrecipRate, QPE, MESH swaths) plus the ALASKA/HAWAII/GUAM/CARIB domains that exist on the bucket today and that no competitor besides HookEcho reads.
8. Real-time Level II from `unidata-nexrad-level2-chunks` (11 to 12 s cadence), which RadarOmega sells as RapidSweep at $84.99/yr.
9. Units, UTC, font scale, alert-type filters and sounds: settings every competitor has and OpenRadar lacks entirely.
10. Accessibility of the panel system: no `role="dialog"`, no focus restore on close, no arrow navigation in the command list.

## Product Map

- Core workflows: open on the last camera and see MRMS within seconds (or the cached last view with its age); scrub or loop two hours plus six of HRRR; zoom past 8 for the nearest site's Level II with tilt and moment; read alerts, tropical, tides, guidance, route weather and storm history in panels; export a PNG or WebM; save four presets, share an `openradar://` link, switch language and theme in place.
- Personas: the desktop user who wants MyRadar without ads or upsells; the hobbyist chaser who wants RadarScope Tier 2 features without a subscription that Windows cannot even buy (radarscope.com.au/guide/windows-managing-your-radarscope-pro-subscription); the coastal user (tides, surge, tropical) that no OSS radar app serves.
- Platforms: Windows only (`tauri.conf.json` targets `["nsis"]`, `scripts/release.mjs:22,71`), which the README badge now says. Tauri 2.11.5, MapLibre GL 6.6.0, React 19.2.8, Vite 8.2.2, all current as of 2026-08-30.
- Integrations: 21 allowlisted hosts in `src-tauri/src/http.rs:16-37`; 15 served through the `cached` scheme (`tiles.rs:24-40`, `src/lib/tileCache.ts:20-36`). Decoders in Rust: `mrms.rs` (GRIB2 DRT 41), `level2.rs` (nexrad-* rc crates), `lightning.rs` (NetCDF-4 via netcdf-reader), `gfs.rs`. Bundled data: `public/hurdat.json` (2.88 MB), `public/tide-stations.json` (313 KB).

## Competitive Landscape

- **Supercell Wx** (MIT, C++/Qt, v0.6.1 2026-07-19, 464 stars). Learn: 9-pane grid with pop-out, per-product persistent thresholds with manual entry, downed-radar exclusion in the site picker, beam-height tooltip with ARL/MSL (#683), radar and overlay opacity (#682), NSIS updater (#684), TPBI to TDJT site-ID cutover handling, HTTP/ONDAS alternate Level II/III endpoints (#662). Its 2026 asks: #691 reorder layers, #685 SPS, #655 KML/KMZ, #638 GeoJSON with placefiles, #617 sound only on tornado upgrade, #290 velocity dealiasing, #206 azimuthal shear, #167 Level III hail/meso/TVS overlays, #480 gate filter, #401 soundings, #613 cache site data. Avoid: the MapTiler/Mapbox key requirement (its top support burden) and Level 2 velocity left aliased.
- **HookEcho** (MIT, Rust, v0.12.0-beta.1 2026-08-26). Closest feature superset: dealiasing, SRV, SCIT tracking with arrival cones, TDS and couplet detection, ProbSevere, CAPE/SRH from RAP, optical-flow 0 to 45 min, lightning-jump rule, replay bundles, MQTT. Learn: composite cell severity score, warning escalation tiers, "reaches saved location in N min". Avoid: one-person surface sprawl and pre-release status.
- **BowEcho** (Apache-2.0, Rust, v0.35.0 2026-08-28, near-weekly releases). Learn: `bowecho-dealias` (separate MIT/Apache crate, region flood-fill plus boundary fold voting, 21,458 to under 200 residual fold boundaries on the 2026-06-09 KEAX derecho), live radial chunks drawn over the previous tilt, pinned inspector with beam height and Vrot, "hide below" declutter with |v| clamp, archived scans auto-loading the warnings valid at that time, Authenticode plus notarized builds with per-asset sha256. Avoid: WRF/CM1/climate-browser sprawl; documented Defender `!ml` false positives on unsigned Rust exes (OpenRadar's signed updater is a real edge).
- **Anvil** (AGPL-3.0, C#, 2026-08-28) and **ClassicRadar** (JS, 2026-08-28). Ideas only: full-volume VAD storm motion, offline PMTiles basemap with state isolation mask, SPC Day 1 to 8 with hatching keyed to the replay window, NST storm attribute table merged with warning tags, 24 h time machine pinned across products.
- **RadrView** (MIT, TS, stalled 2026-06-19). Same z2-7 MRMS to z8+ Level II handoff; biological echo layer from CC; rotating sweep wedge over WebSocket.
- **LibreWXR** (AGPL, server). Network use only: 13 colour schemes, optical-flow interpolation and 60-min nowcast, OPERA/DWD/GeoMet/MARN sources. Its #24 shows nowcast blending erasing precipitation, so keep pure extrapolation as an option if a nowcast lands.
- **RadarScope** (Windows $29.99 + Tier 1 $9.99/yr + Tier 2 $99.99/yr, Tier 2 not purchasable on Windows). Paywalls: non-gridded lightning, 30-frame Level II loops, dual pane (Tier 1); 30-day archive, shear contours, hail contours, SPC Day 1, LSRs (Tier 2). 5.6 (2026-08) added warning impact tags. Windows pain: startup crashes with crash dumps by email, "Get Location" 10 miles off.
- **RadarOmega** ($8.99 base; Gamma $49/yr unlocks desktop; Beta $89 for MRMS hail/rotation; Alpha $119 for models; RapidSweep $84.99/yr for near-real-time scans). Desktop itself is gated behind a subscription. Reviews: 3D lag, tiny fonts, refresh interrupting the loop.
- **GRLevelX** (GR2Analyst 3 $250, GRLevel3 $79). The placefile ecosystem is the moat: PlacefileNation serves 54 free endpoints for GRLevelX, WSV3 and Supercell Wx; placefiles rot silently after a machine move (stormtrack 31427).
- **Storm Radar (TWC)**, relaunched 2026-03-31, $19.99/yr: storm cells with speed, direction and "when they're expected to reach you", 72 h US future radar. Reviews punish the removed on-map measuring tool.
- **MyRadar** Pro: per-station radar with tilt, hurricane cone with spaghetti models, 5-year archive, RouteCast, webhooks for alerts (7.121), live PTZ camera layer. Windows entitlements vanish after install (freshdesk 44002350309).
- **Windy** (no native desktop; Premium $34.99/yr): 1-hour radar forecast frustrates paying users (topic 42998); forum asks for single-station selection because "composite has noise" and "RadarOmega exists but is expensive" (topic 33313); volumetric 3D with a hardware-detect toggle (topic 44855).
- **Baron Threat Net / Lynx**: times-of-arrival boxes, storm vectors with "tornado likely/possible" flags, telestration. **Zoom Earth** 6.1: crosshair readout while panning. **CARROT** Ultra $39.99/yr gates lightning, alerts, adjustable loop start/end. **Ventusky** raised Premium 4x in 2026; price shock is the recurring community theme.
- Adjacent UX worth copying: ForeFlight per-layer opacity sliders with the legend docked above the time slider; OpenCPN GRIB "Now" button and loop-from-now; Flightradar24 zoomable playback timeline with speed; Blitzortung strike-age ramp in 20-minute bands.

## Security, Privacy, and Reliability

- Fixed 2026-08-30: `https://assets.openfreemap.com` and `https://*.openfreemap.org` were removed from `connect-src`, `img-src` and `font-src`, and `src/lib/csp.test.ts` now reads the policy back the other way as well.
- `router.project-osrm.org` (`src/lib/route.ts:4`): the OSRM demo server wiki says "restricted to reasonable, non-commercial use-cases", "do not exceed 1 request per second", "no guarantees wrt. uptime". The app is non-commercial, but there is no client-side rate guard and no user-facing statement of the dependency beyond `en.ts:129`. Verified.
- OpenTopoMap credit line is exact (`mapStyles.ts:123`) and Open-Meteo is credited (`en.ts:32,63,129`); Open-Meteo free tier is 10,000 calls/day, 5,000/hour, 600/minute, CC-BY 4.0, non-commercial (open-meteo.com/en/terms). The guidance panel issues one multi-model call per map move; keep it debounced.
- `cargo audit` 2026-08-30: 0 vulnerabilities, 18 warnings, all unmaintained or unsound transitives (gtk-rs 0.18 bindings via tauri on Linux, `unic-*` via a tauri dependency, `glib` RUSTSEC-2024-0429, `lru` 0.16.4 RUSTSEC-2026-0253 panic-safety in `pop()`, `proc-macro-error`). None is reachable on Windows. `npm audit`: 0 vulnerabilities. GitHub advisory query for `tauri` on the `rust` ecosystem: newest is GHSA-7gmj-67g7-phm9 (2026-05-06, origin confusion on Windows), fixed in 2.11.1; the app runs 2.11.5. Verified.
- Vite 8 dev-server advisories in 2026 (GHSA-fx2h-pf6j-xcff, GHSA-v2wj-q39q-566r, GHSA-p9ff-h696-f583) are all Windows file-read holes in the dev server; 8.2.2 clears them. Vitest 4.1.11 clears GHSA-5xrq-8626-4rwp. Never run `vitest --ui` on an exposed interface. Verified.
- Level II format change: SCN26-54 adds an LTR (long-term average reflectivity) message to the Level II stream hourly from about 2027-02-15 (Build 25.0, testbed KCRI already emits it). The decoder pins `nexrad-decode =1.0.0-rc.3`; an unknown message type must be skipped, not fatal. Needs live validation against KCRI.
- SCN26-67 moves the NOMADS Level II HTTPS pull to `tgftp.nws.noaa.gov` on 2026-09-15. OpenRadar reads AWS only (`level2.rs:216`), so it is unaffected. Verified.
- Fixed 2026-08-30: the nearest few sites in reach are asked whether the archive holds a volume from the last twenty minutes, and the first that does takes the view.
- Fixed 2026-08-30: the survey's claim that thirteen library fetches took no abort signal was wrong. Every one already accepted and forwarded a signal; the two that do not, `hurdat.ts:82` and `tides.ts:48`, are shared loaders of bundled files and deliberately take none. What was missing was any test, which `src/lib/abort.test.ts` now supplies.
- Two timers without cleanup: `src/hooks/useToasts.ts:30` and `src/hooks/useWorkspaceActions.ts:201`. Verified.
- Undo gaps: `onClearPalette` (`App.tsx:397`) and `onStopReplay` clear state with no undo toast; every other destructive action has one. Verified.
- LICENSE names "Matt @ Maven Imaging" while `Cargo.toml` and git author are `SysAdminDoc`; the global rule says never brand with that company name. Verified.
- No settings export or import; a reinstall loses presets and the watched place. Verified (`UtilityPanels.tsx:41-51`, `settings.ts:378-397`).
- MapLibre 6 requires WebGL2 and there is no capability check before `new Map` (`src/main.tsx`, `src/components/MapViewport.tsx`, `ErrorBoundary.tsx` contain no `webgl` probe); on a GPU-less VM or a WebView2 with hardware acceleration off the app throws into the boundary with a generic message. Verified in code; https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/guides/v5-to-v6-migration-guide/
- Windows webview origin: Tauri 2 serves production from `http://tauri.localhost`; flipping `app.windows[].useHttpsScheme` in a later release changes the origin and orphans localStorage and IndexedDB. Settings already live in the store plugin (`settings.ts`), so only browser-preview state is exposed; the decision should still be recorded as fixed. Likely; https://tauri.app/start/migrate/from-tauri-1/
- `alerts.weather.gov` ATOM/CAP feeds were decommissioned 2025-12-02 (SCN 25-73); the app reads the event-driven ArcGIS service and `api.weather.gov`, so it is unaffected. Verified; https://www.weather.gov/idp/alertsdecom
- reqwest is built `rustls`, no default features, so the six 2026 rust-openssl advisories (GHSA-xp3w-r5p5-63rr and siblings) never enter the Windows build. Verified in `Cargo.toml`.
- Open-Meteo shipped a Single Runs API (2026-04-02) and an Ensemble Mean API (2026-03) on the same free tier; the guidance panel's `models=` call could move to single runs to show run time per model. Verified; https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/single-runs-api

## Architecture Assessment

- `src/components/MapViewport.tsx` is 1,365 lines with no `useMemo`/`useCallback` and no tests; every overlay's `setData` lives there (`:388,575,650,769,936,1012`). Split per adapter the way `src/lib/overlays/` already is, so a layer's MapLibre spec and its data push live together.
- `src/hooks/useLightning.ts:44-60` rebuilds the whole flash FeatureCollection on every clock tick; bucket by age once per fetch and let a data-driven `opacity` expression on `age` do the fading.
- `src/lib/hurdat.ts:82-87` fetches and parses 2.88 MB synchronously on first History open with no abort; gzip it at build time (`scripts/build-hurdat.mjs`) and parse in a worker, or index by year and load lazily.
- Dead exports: `src/lib/log.ts:61 resetLog`, `src/lib/hurdat.ts:96 resetStorms`, `src/lib/level2.ts:30 level2ProductLabel`.
- i18n lint gap: `src/App.tsx:443-445` toasts "Dual pane closed" / "Dual pane opened" as literals; `coverage.test.ts` scans `hooks/`, `lib/`, `panels/`, `components/` and `App.tsx` but its `set` pattern needs eight characters after a capital and a space, which these satisfy, so the miss is the `toast(` call shape. Extend the lint to `toast({ title:` and `addToast(`.
- Accessibility: only four `role=` attributes app-wide; panels are not `role="dialog"`, have no `aria-modal`, and no focus restore on close; the command palette list has no arrow navigation (`CommandPalette.tsx`). `e2e/accessibility.spec.ts` runs axe on 11 panels, which does not catch focus loss.
- Settings gaps: no units (imperial/metric), no time zone (local/UTC), no font scale, no per-alert-type filter, no sounds. `src/lib/settings.ts` schema 2 has 12 top-level keys; every key is read. Windows window size and position are not persisted (`tauri-plugin-window-state` 2.4.1 is not a dependency).
- Theme and basemap are independent: switching to Light in Settings keeps the dark basemap (`useSettings.ts:106-114` only sets `data-theme`); the v0.2.0 screenshots show white panels over a black map.
- Tests: 40 vitest files (262 tests), 16 e2e specs (66 tests across two viewports), 89 Rust tests. No tests for `MapViewport.tsx`, `windLayer.ts`, `useAlertWatch`, `useExport`, `useUpdates`, `useWind`, `useWorkspaceActions`, `providers/{budget,health,nowcoast,ridge}`, `lib.rs`.
- Docs drift: `README.md:5` platform badge; `docs/architecture.md:3` still says Rust "can later decode NEXRAD and MRMS"; `docs/asset-ledger.md` lists six assets and stops at RainViewer while 21 hosts are allowlisted (OpenTopoMap CC-BY-SA and NASA GIBS acknowledgement are obligations); `design-qa.md` references `artifacts/` (gitignored) and a local `myradar-audit` path and predates every v0.2.0 panel.

## Rejected Ideas

- Blitzortung direct feed: contact page (2026) still requires your own relay, forbids warning use; GLM and MRMS NLDN density already cover it. Source: blitzortung.org/en/contact.php.
- RainViewer nowcast, `@openmeteo/weather-map-layer` (GPL-2.0), weatherlayers-gl (MPL plus commercial), LibreWXR/Anvil/AtticRadar/OpenStorm code reuse (AGPL, AGPL, unlicensed, GPL-2): licence. Ideas only.
- Third-party CORS proxy or a hosted relay: AtticRadar's #39/#27 show it as the top failure mode; all fetches stay direct from the machine.
- Cloud accounts, sync, telemetry, webhooks to third-party services (MyRadar 7.121): contradicts "Private by default" in Diagnostics; a local ntfy-style push could be revisited only as opt-in with a user-supplied URL.
- 9-pane grid and pop-out windows (Supercell v0.6.0): the 1024x680 minimum window and the command bar make a third pane unreadable; dual pane stays.
- 3D volumetric rendering (GR2Analyst, RadarOmega, OpenStorm, Windy topic 44855): needs the full 3D cube per volume and a WebGL volume renderer; RadarOmega's own reviews call it laggy. Cross-section (RHI) is the cheaper half and stays Under Consideration.
- Skew-T soundings from RAP (BowEcho v0.34, Supercell #401): RAP is not on the allowlist, Open-Meteo pressure levels are model output not observations; Later, not Next.
- Keyboard shortcuts (F11, Ctrl+K): global rule. Arrow keys inside a listbox and Escape on a dialog are WAI-ARIA pattern behaviour, listed as an open question below.
- Mobile, Wear, iframe embed mode (StormviewRadar): desktop-only by design; ZeusWatch covers the Android lane.
- GitHub Actions builds, winget manifests, Dependabot: global rules.
- RRFS as a future-radar source before 2026-10-06: HRRR is not retired by SCN26-47 (REFS ingests two HRRR members), IEM serves no RRFS tiles yet; re-check after RRFS v2 is dated.
- Esri World Imagery, MapTiler, Mapbox: keyed. The USGS National Map orthoimagery already serves the aerial style.
